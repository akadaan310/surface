## Global Constraints

- Preserve original archives and evidence; no private keys in source.
- Native renderer only; no browser product substitution.
- At most 300 seconds per supervised job and 120 seconds without semantic progress; heartbeat never renews deadline.
- One classified retry at most, within the original deadline.
- Exact UTF-8 source bytes, half-open byte spans and explicit semantic mapping.
- Scientific ACCEPT requires evidence; compile/run success does not complete original science.

## Review Focus

- Unicode, CRLF and repeated text: exact source map validation, no byte-offset/character-index confusion.
- Unknown clauses and negation: draft remains unreviewed; never auto-execute inferred intent.
- Cycles, unknown adapters and excessive budgets: fail before process launch.
- Existing evidence/output paths: new runs use fenced generations; no historical result replacement.
- Upstream resemblance: preserve commit/file hashes and scope; test actual imported code.
