# Photographic observation fixture

`HorseHead.fits` is retained byte-for-byte from the Astropy/STScI data server for scientific research and independent FITS/WCS conformance. It is an image from a digitized UK Schmidt photographic plate, with original attribution `AAO/ROE` preserved in its header.

Acknowledgment: the Digitized Sky Surveys were produced by the Space Telescope Science Institute with support under U.S. Government grant NAG W-2166. This image's photographic source is the UK Schmidt Telescope; original plate attribution and institution identities remain in the FITS source and provenance record. STScI digitization and distribution through the Astropy data collection are acknowledged.

[MAST's data-use policy](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use) identifies DSS material as copyrighted. Research, teaching and nonprofit use is permitted with requested source acknowledgment. Commercial use requires written permission. The data is not relabelled under a software license. [DSS acknowledgment guidance](https://archive.stsci.edu/dss/acknowledging.html).

`HorseHead.download.json` records the actual acquisition. `HorseHead.provenance.json` records scientific interpretation, rights, source and mirror identity. The FITS source is not a calibrated CCD exposure, and no flux or noise model is asserted.
